Evaluation trajectories

Every run behind the paper, at full sample count — not a sample of them. Click any method name to open its trajectories: the injected seed with its ranked documents, each reasoning and observation step, every search the agent issued with what came back, the final answer and the judge's verdict.

BrowseComp+ traces are withheld. Its authors ask that the benchmark never appear as plain text online, so we do not publish its corpus, questions or trajectories. MuSiQue and FanOutQA carry no such request and are shown in full. A gated release for BrowseComp+ is in preparation.

What is in the payload

  • Documents are stored once and referenced by corpus id, so a document that appears in many trajectories is downloaded once.
  • Retrieved documents are marked gold or evidence; everything else is a distractor. BrowseComp+ and FanOutQA use the benchmarks' official qrels; MuSiQue has no qrels, so its gold documents come from the supporting-paragraph annotations and it has no separate evidence tier.
  • Every released run is scored. Where a score is genuinely unavailable the table shows – rather than a number, and those samples carry no verdict instead of being counted as wrong.
