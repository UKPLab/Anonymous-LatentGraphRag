What is in the payload

  • Each trajectory keeps the full record: the starting evidence, every turn the model produced, every search with the five documents it returned, the final answer and its grade.
  • Documents are stored once and referenced by corpus id, so a document that appears in many trajectories is downloaded once.
  • Documents are marked gold or evidence; everything else is a distractor. FanOutQA uses the benchmark's official qrels; MuSiQue has no qrels, so its gold documents are the supporting paragraphs of each question and it has no separate evidence tier.
