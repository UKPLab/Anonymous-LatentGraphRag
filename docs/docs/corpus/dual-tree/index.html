Dual-Tree

Two parallel trees — one over documents (bD), one over memories (bM). After expansion, both views are projected to document-level rankings and fused. The strongest MuSiQue R@10 (90.36) comes from this mode.

Parameters

NameTypeDefaultDescription

Why two trees?

Documents and memories surface different signal. Documents preserve context; memories preserve relations. Running both views in parallel and fusing means the final ranking is robust to either failing on a particular query.

Canonical configurations

  • MMR-Tree + Z-Agreement (best mean R@5 44.65 and R@10 54.26): search="mmr_tree", fusion="z_agreement", b_D=3, b_M=5.
  • Dual RRF, Full Expansion (best calibration-free fusion): fusion="rrf", b_D=3, b_M=5.
  • Full Dual-Tree + Agreement (best MuSiQue R@10): fusion="agreement", b_D=3, b_M=5, γ=0.
